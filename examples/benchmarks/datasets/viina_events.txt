Regional officials said the station was packed with around 2,000 people when a Tochka‑U ballistic missile carrying a cluster munition warhead detonated on the main platform at about 10:30 a.m. local time. Remnants of the missile, including an engine section with Russian inscriptions, were recovered near the tracks and examined by specialists from Ukraine’s 90th Separate Airmobile Battalion. Kyiv said satellite imagery indicated the launch originated from territory near occupied Shakhtarsk, where units of Russia’s 8th Combined Arms Army were operating. Moscow denied responsibility, claiming without evidence that Ukraine’s 19th Missile Brigade fired the weapon. Independent weapons analysts told Reuters the fragments matched a Tochka‑U variant used extensively by Russian forces in early 2022.

ZAPORIZHZHIA, Jan. 17, 2023 – International nuclear experts on Tuesday demanded an investigation into alleged Russian mistreatment of Ukrainian staff at the occupied Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant in Enerhodar, calling the reports potential war crimes. Ukraine’s Energoatom agency said at least 33 employees had been detained since August 2022 by Russian forces from the 22nd Army Corps and Rosgvardiya units guarding the facility. Former detainees told Ukrainian prosecutors they were held in the plant’s disused turbine hall and beaten with rifle butts, subjected to electric shocks from field telephones and threatened with execution to force them to sign contracts with Russia’s state operator Rosenergoatom. One senior engineer, 43‑year‑old Oleksandr R., died on Oct. 10 after what colleagues described as a prolonged interrogation. The International Atomic Energy Agency, which maintains a small monitoring team on site, said it had received “consistent and worrying” testimonies but lacked full access to detention areas. Russia’s RIA news agency quoted occupation officials denying any abuse, saying security measures were “strict but lawful”.

KYIV, March 14, 2023 (Reuters) - Ukrainian engineers from the 47th Separate Mechanized Brigade have accelerated work on a new defensive belt around the eastern city of Kramatorsk, officials said on Tuesday, as Russian forces press their offensive near Bakhmut and Avdiivka.
